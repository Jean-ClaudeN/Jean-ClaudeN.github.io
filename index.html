What I bring

Research on trust and adoption

My thesis research covers producers and agriculture students across eleven Midwestern states, using established adoption models. I know how to design the study, work with farm audiences, and turn the results into decisions for tool builders.

Field credibility

I have worked directly with smallholder cooperatives and farmers on mechanization, irrigation and technology adoption, and I now run sensor-based irrigation research on 15 urban farms in Lincoln and Omaha.

Builder and connector

I built the AgiriteChat advisory prototype, co-founded Agirite, and speak on AI in agriculture at university, industry and international forums, including a Google-backed fellowship cohort and Black in AI.

About

I am a graduate student and researcher at the University of Nebraska–Lincoln, advised by Dr. Santosh Pitla. My first degree is a B.Sc. in Conservation Agriculture, specializing in irrigation and mechanized agriculture, from the Rwanda Institute for Conservation Agriculture.

Before graduate school I worked with farmer cooperatives in Africa on technology adoption, mechanization and irrigation, and I co-founded Agirite, a social enterprise connecting smallholder farmers to markets, training and financial services.

I am a Graduate Research Assistant on the USDA-NIFA funded Cultivate ACCESS to Data Science in Agriculture project.
